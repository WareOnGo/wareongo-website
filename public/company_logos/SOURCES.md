# Company logo sources

Retrieved 2026-09-26. Logos are stored locally so the carousel does not depend on
third-party image requests. Original artwork is preserved; raster files have
been cropped to their artwork bounds and encoded as lossless WebP. SVG path
data comes from the sources below. Responsive raster variants are generated by
`scripts/generate-logo-variants.mjs`.

The shared carousel keeps the existing companies and includes all 19 requested
brands, with one entry each. Groups of three or four larger, established brands
are followed by one smaller brand, spreading the smaller businesses throughout
the loop. Business scale is approximate and includes the groups behind major
brands; it is not a precise comparison of net worth or company valuation.
